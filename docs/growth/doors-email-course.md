# The 10 Doors — Email Course Content (`doors-email-course`)

> Capture page (LIVE): https://www.astrokalki.com/email-course
> Subscribe API: POST /api/email-course/subscribe (attribution-stamped, honeypot, rate-limited)
> Admin: /admin/subscribers (list + CSV export). War Room: "Email course" panel.

## 1. Delivery mechanics

- **Cadence:** one email/day for 10 days, sent 8:00–9:00pm IST (the publishing hour —
  consistency beats optimality). Welcome email sends immediately on subscribe.
- **From name:** Kaustubh — AstroKalki. **Reply-to:** a real inbox you read.
- **Subject line conventions:** short, verb-first, one open loop. A/B only the welcome subject.
- Every email ends with ONE CTA. Conversion emails (D1, D5, D10) CTA to
  `/consultations`; others may soft-CTA the related pattern/folio page.

## 2. Attribution convention (War Room-ready)

Every link inside every email carries:
```
utm_source=email&utm_medium=course&utm_campaign=doors-email-course&utm_content=day-N
```
Day-N rolls up in the War Room door board next to `file-N` and `door-N`.
Geo: append `utm_term` only in geo-split future variants — not needed at this volume.

## 4. The ten doors (send in this order)

### DAY 1 — Kālī — subject: "The ending you keep reopening"
**Loop:** endings you refuse to accept — the ex you check, the job you mourn, the
friendship you keep resuscitating.
**Body sketch (expand to ~180 words):** Kālimā wears the ending as a garland. Not to
celebrate death — to make it visible. In your life, the ending already happened; what
continues is the reopening. Every check of their profile is a resurrection attempt.
The Tantric read: Kālī doesn't end things. She ends the ILLUSION that endings are
optional. CTA: tonight's one line — what ending are you still reopening?
**[CONVERSION — CTA: when the same loop has run three or more rounds, it needs a
diagnosis, not another attempt → /consultations, utm_content=day-1]**

### DAY 2 — Tārā — subject: "Why chaos feels like home"
**Loop:** manufacturing urgency — drama, deadlines, crisis — because calm feels
unfamiliar. Tārā is the navigator who walks through chaos without drinking it.
**CTA:** name the last time you were bored and peaceful for a full hour. If you
can't — that's the file.

### DAY 3 — Tripura Sundarī — subject: "The desire that never fills"
**Loop:** consumption as a cure — the buy, the scroll, the next plan — the feeling
that the missing thing is one purchase away. Sundarī is not the desire; she is what
remains when desire stops running.
**CTA:** the last thing you acquired — write the feeling you hoped it would produce.

### DAY 4 — Bhuvaneśvarī — subject: "Control is a room with no windows"
**Loop:** controlling people/outcomes/schedules because space feels like danger.
Bhuvaneśvarī IS space — the goddess of the room, not the furniture.
**CTA:** one thing you will deliberately not control tomorrow. Watch what actually happens.

### DAY 5 — Bhairavī — subject: "The anger you swallow comes back wearing your face" — **[CONVERSION]**
**Loop:** swallowed anger → reflux as sarcasm, body symptoms, "mood". Bhairavī is the
fire that digests, not destroys.
**Body sketch:** she is what your unspoken sentence looks like when it stops asking
permission. The tantric line: suppressed fire doesn't disappear; it changes address —
it moves into the jaw, the gut, the reply you almost sent.
**CTA:** if the anger has an address in your body, one session maps it to its source → /consultations, utm_content=day-5

### DAY 6 — Chhinnamastā — subject: "You give until you disappear"
**Loop:** over-giving — the friend/parent/colleague who funds everyone's life with
their own blood. Chhinnamastā gives her own head — the file about giving that has cut
off the giver.
**CTA:** the last time someone saw YOUR need. Write the date. If it's blank — that's the finding.

### DAY 7 — Dhūmāvatī — subject: "The emptiness you keep busy to avoid"
**Loop:** busyness as anesthesia — the calendar as a hiding place. Dhūmāvatī is the
goddess of the empty room, and she is the most misunderstood of the ten: she doesn't
bring the emptiness, she sits IN it until you can too.
**CTA:** sit in an empty room, no screen, ten minutes. Write what arrives in minute seven.

### DAY 8 — Bagalāmukhī — subject: "Starting and stopping is one pattern, not two"
**Loop:** the freeze — projects at 70%, gym at week three. Bagalāmukhī is the power of
the STILL point — she freezes what should be frozen. Your problem is she's operating
on the wrong targets.
**CTA:** list three things frozen at 70%. One of them deserves the freeze. The other two are waiting.

### DAY 9 — Mātaṅgī — subject: "You've been rehearsing silence"
**Loop:** the swallowed voice — the opinion edited out, the boundary softened into a
hint. Mātaṅgī is the outcaste goddess — she speaks from outside the court, and her
speech REMAKES the court.
**CTA:** the sentence you edited out this week. Say it today — exactly as it was written.

### DAY 10 — Kamalā — subject: "The worthiness invoice (collect it)" — **[CONVERSION — strongest]**
**Loop:** the discounting reflex — undercharging, over-earning-for-others, the "who am
I to..." tax on every ambition. Kamalā doesn't CREATE worth; she sits in it — the
lotus doesn't apologize for the mud it rose from.
**Body sketch:** ten days ago you started collecting data. Look at your notes: every
"this is where it happens in my life" line is an invoice for worthiness you never
submitted. Kamalā is the collector's energy — not greed, but ACCURACY about value.
You've seen all ten loops now. Most people see one clearly — the one that runs them.
**CTA:** if one loop is now unmistakable, don't let it wait for another cycle. The
Mirror Method names it, maps it, and hands you the sequence out — one session. →
/consultations, utm_content=day-10 (subject-line A/B: "The worthiness invoice" vs "Door 10 — and the one loop that's been running you")

## 7. Activating automated delivery (when ready)

The capture layer is live with or without a sender. To auto-send:

1. Create a Resend account → verify sending domain `astrokalki.com` (DKIM records → Hostinger DNS).
2. `RESEND_API_KEY` env var in Vercel → wire into a scheduled sender:
   - a Vercel Cron hitting `/api/cron/course-send` (to be built when key exists —
     NOT built now, deliberately, so the freeze stays intact) → picks active
     subscribers, computes their current day by `createdAt`, sends the matching
     email from this doc, logs send state.
3. Until then: export CSV weekly from /admin/subscribers and send the day's email
   manually (BCC batch is fine under ~100 subscribers).

**Do not** move to a third-party capture form — the first-party attribution stamp
(UTM + country + landing path) is the entire point of this build.
