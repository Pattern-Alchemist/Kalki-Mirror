### Guardrails (read before shooting)

1. **Myth as mirror, not ritual.** Never prescribe puja steps, mantra
   instructions, or "worship her like this". We do pattern psychology through
   the goddesses' iconography. This keeps us on the right side of guru-theatre
   AND of the evidence-graded house style.
2. **Respect bhakti.** The critique always targets a *pattern*, never a
   deity, a tradition, or devotees. Devout viewers should feel honored, not
   mocked — they're a conversion segment too.
3. **No manifestation promises.** No "universe will deliver". Interventions
   are behavioral (write it down, send the invoice, freeze for 6 hours).
4. **Account discipline.** All IG traffic routes to **@unfilteredbuddy_**.
   The discarded personal account is never referenced, tagged, or hinted at.
5. **Verdict discipline.** These shorts are pattern-work, so verdicts
   (Attested/Debunked etc.) stay out of frame — that's GUHYA's language.
   The one line that IS allowed: "Evidence-graded platform: what we cannot
   verify, we mark" (in the description footer).
