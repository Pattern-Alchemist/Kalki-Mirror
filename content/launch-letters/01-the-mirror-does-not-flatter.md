The mirror does not flatter

You have probably noticed that most advice about the mind begins with a compliment. You are powerful. You are unlimited. You are the universe folded into a body, waiting to unfold. These letters will not begin that way. The Mirror begins with something rarer: attention.

## The pattern you can test today

There is a pattern we call the Witness — the part of you that watches without stepping in. Most people have never met it directly, because the moment observation begins, another character grabs the microphone. The Pleaser, for instance. It watches nothing; it scans. It reads the room and asks one question: what must I become so that no one is disappointed?

Scanning feels like awareness. It is not. Awareness has no agenda. Scanning has a hundred.

## The exercise

For one week, once a day, notice the difference in the body:

- When you are scanning: a low hum behind the sternum, a readiness to adjust yourself mid-sentence.
- When you are witnessing: weight in the feet, breath in the belly, no rehearsal running.

Do not fix anything. The instruction is only to tell the difference. The Mirror does not flatter and it does not condemn — it reports.
