One flame, one thought

Attention is the poorest person you know — always split among a dozen landlords, owing everyone. The corpus has a technology for this, older than any of the machines currently competing for your eyes, and it costs the price of one candle.

## Trataka, stated plainly

Trataka is steady gazing: one small point — traditionally a flame at eye level — held by the eyes until the eyes water slightly or the mind goes unusually quiet, whichever arrives first. Then close the eyes and keep the afterimage. That is the whole mechanism. The afterimage is not the goal; it is the proof that attention held long enough to leave a groove.

## The honest cautions

- Moderately, not heroically — a few minutes is a complete session. Strain is not devotion.
- Soft lenses and hard nights both disqualify you for the day; the flame will wait.
- If the practice produces headache rather than quiet, you are forcing. The corpus is consistent about this: force spoils the instrument it tries to clean.

## Why a flame and not a screen

A screen flickers, scrolls, and interrupts by design; it trains the scan. A flame does nothing. It cannot compete for you, and that is precisely why it wins. The pattern we call the Void — the low-grade famine of never having attended to anything for more than nine seconds — is fed by exactly this kind of starving: one fixed point, held.
